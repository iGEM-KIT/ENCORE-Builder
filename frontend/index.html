ENCORE Builder

From coding sequence to an assembly-ready nanocompartment-cargo construct.

The ENCORE Builder turns your gene of interest into a complete encapsulin cargo construct: configure the nanocompartment site, enter your cargo with the modules around it, and choose the promoter; the builder returns the standardized Golden Gate modules, cargo primers, and the final Level 2 plasmid.

        Step 5

        Overview

        What to order as oligos or primers, and the sequences as DNA. Your own sequences are codon-optimized for S. cerevisiae; the sequences below are the optimized ones.

        Complete step 4 first.

        Every part shorter than 150 bp (your own sequence or a toolbox part) is ordered as an oligo pair: order the two oligos, anneal them and ligate the product into the Level 0 backbone; the 5′ overhangs already fit, no digest needed.

        To build your own sequences into the toolbox: PCR them with these primers (annealing part plus BbsI site, overhangs and frame spacer), then clone the product into the Level 0 backbone with BbsI.

        Every part with a real sequence that is not ordered as oligos, as one double-stranded fragment (adapter with BbsI sites, overhangs and frame spacer around the part). Order it as DNA instead of PCR with primers, and clone it with BbsI into the Level 0 backbone.
