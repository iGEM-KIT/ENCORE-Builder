ENCORE Builder

From coding sequence to an assembly-ready nanocompartment–cargo construct.

The ENCORE Builder turns your gene of interest into a complete encapsulin cargo construct: configure the nanocompartment site, enter your cargo with the modules around it, and choose the promoter — the builder returns the standardized Golden Gate modules, cargo primers, and the final Level 2 plasmid.

Level 0

The individual parts of your cargo module

4 from toolkit stock · 1 new

Level 0 parts are cloned separately — one plasmid each. They are not joined yet, and the row above is not a strand: the Type number alone fixes where each part ends up once BsaI assembles them into the Level 1 module. Only your cargo is new and needs BbsI domestication; the other four come off the stock plate.

Level 2

Finished construct

Cargo ← Promoter → NC
Both transcripts start (5′) at the central promoter and run outward (3′): the NC module on the sense (+) strand (right), the cargo module on the antisense (−) strand (left). The four modules around the shell (Type 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.5) can be NCS spacers, linkers, display markers — e.g. SpyCatcher, displayed on the encapsulin exterior — or your own sequences. A * marks the part carrying the stop codon (each ORF's 3′ end); its position follows the C/N fusion and the modules next to the shell.
