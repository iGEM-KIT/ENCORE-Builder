OpenDyslexic

The four font files in this folder (opendyslexic-regular, bold, italic and bolditalic, WOFF2) are the OpenDyslexic typeface
(https://opendyslexic.org). OpenDyslexic is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The license allows to use,
study, copy, merge, embed, modify, redistribute and sell the fonts, as long as they are not sold by themselves and this notice
stays with them. The full text of the license is at https://openfontlicense.org.

The files were taken from the toolKIT wiki of iGEM Karlsruhe 2026 (static.igem.wiki, team 6296), where the same dyslexia-friendly
font mode is offered.
